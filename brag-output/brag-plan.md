# WhyLog — brag plan (60s cinematic)

## Angle

Immune system, not memory. The question is not “what did we decide?” but **is what we decided still true?**

## Hook (first 2s)

Dark surface. WhyLog wordmark. The question lands before any product UI.

## Highlights (2–3)

1. **Premises under the decision** — SQLite card with RESTS ON / IN labels (the *given*).
2. **Gate (c) cascade** — world moves (four users); premise goes OUT; lattice cascades. Peak line.
3. **Close the loop** — guards + “Rejected — do not re-propose” export.

## Punchline

*Memory tools stop you repeating yourself. WhyLog stops you building on a decision that stopped being true.*

## Tone

`cinematic` — epic type, soft dramatic transitions, long hold on the cascade.

## Visual identity

- Surface `#0b0d10` / raised `#12151a` / text `#e6e9ee` / dim `#99a1ad`
- IN `#4ade80` · OUT `#94a3b8` · UNKNOWN `#fbbf24` · action `#f87171`
- Logos from `work/assets/`; epistemic colour encodes status, never severity

## Share caption (draft)

WhyLog is a truth maintenance system for decisions you make with AI coding agents. It stores the premises those decisions rest on — and when the world moves without anyone contradicting you, the log notices.

## One-line creative angle

Gate-(c) premise invalidation as the money shot: the world moves, the lattice cascades, nothing was contradicted.
